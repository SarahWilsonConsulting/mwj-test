Executive Consulting

Executive Consulting for Peer Recovery, Behavioral Health and Reentry Programs

Michael advises executive directors, program managers and funders who want peer recovery and reentry programs that hold up under real pressure. Each engagement starts with what your organization already has and what your funding requires. Michael also works as a nonprofit program development consultant for community and faith-based organizations building new programs.

Peer workforce development

Plan, launch or strengthen a peer recovery program. Peer recovery support services are non-clinical services delivered by trained people with lived experience of recovery. Michael helps you define peer roles, supervision, workflows and the training path your staff need.

Peer recovery support services technical assistance

Hands-on help with a specific problem in your peer program, such as unclear peer roles, staff turnover or boundary concerns. Technical assistance means an expert works alongside your team for a set period to solve it.

Recovery community organization development

Support for organizations led by people in recovery, known as recovery community organizations (RCOs), on structure, programs and leadership.

Reentry program development

Build, design or review programs for returning citizens, the people coming home from prison or jail. That includes intake, engagement, peer mentoring and links to recovery support. Michael has been through reentry himself, so he knows where participants tend to fall away.

Bring the Minor Adjustments series to your organization

An 8-week group series built on the Minor Adjustments method, delivered in colleges, correctional facilities and workforce programs. Participants learn to notice unhelpful thinking patterns and replace them with practical, everyday changes. Engagements include facilitation, pre- and post-cohort surveys and a written summary for your leadership or funders. [CONFIRM: what the surveys measure] The series is education, not treatment.

Leadership Training

Leadership Training for Peer Recovery, Behavioral Health and Reentry Teams

Training for the people who lead, supervise and support peer staff, case managers and frontline teams. It builds on the certification training your peer staff already have and focuses on what comes next: leading well, supervising well and keeping good people.

Who it is for: executive directors, peer supervisors, clinical and case management leads, probation and reentry staff, and second-chance and recovery-friendly employers who hire people in recovery or coming home from incarceration.

Training topics

  • Peer leadership trainingFor peer staff moving into lead and supervisory roles.

  • De-escalationCalm, practical responses when a conversation turns tense.

Accountability Training for Employees and Business Teams

This offer began with a request from a business owner who saw how Michael's team works. [CONFIRM: OK to mention] The training is for your team and for you and your leads, because accountability works when everyone knows their part.

How do I get employees to care?
People care more when they know what is expected, why it matters and that someone will follow up. The training gives your team that clarity and gives you a simple way to follow up.
How do I hold employees accountable without babysitting?
Set clear expectations, check in on a schedule, and give a lead the authority to act when you are away. Michael works with you and your leads on all three.
How do I deal with lazy employees?
Start by checking what looks like laziness. More often, expectations are unclear or follow-up is weak. The training addresses both and helps you see which one you are dealing with.
How do I motivate lazy employees?
Motivation gets people started; clear expectations and steady follow-up keep them going. Michael helps your crew or staff take ownership of their work, so you are not the only one pushing.
